💡 Mobile Background Tracking Guide:
1. Floating Mini-Tracker (Recommended): Tap the button above to pop out a floating HUD. As long as it floats in the corner of your phone screen, Android keeps GPS tracking alive while you use WhatsApp, Maps, or other apps!
2. Screen Wake Lock: Automatically active while tracking to keep your screen awake and prevent phone sleep.
3. Instant Catch-up: If you minimize or lock, the moment you unlock or switch back, fresh GPS is instantly captured and synced with the server.
4. Install as App (PWA): Tap browser menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" or "Install App" to run in standalone high-priority mode.
